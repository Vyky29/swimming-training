MODULE 1 - Foundations of Aquatic Skill Development

This module is the starting point of the training pathway.

After this module, instructors will understand how swimmers experience the aquatic environment and how to interpret their responses before applying structured teaching approaches in Module 2 - Guiding Learning Through Scaffolding.

Module Overview

Module Overview

This module introduces the foundations of aquatic development by focusing on how swimmers experience, respond to, and engage with the aquatic environment.

Rather than prioritising structured movement or stroke performance, this module focuses on behaviour, regulation, readiness and control as the foundations of learning in water.

Aquatic development is understood as a progressive process where confidence, stability, engagement and independence are developed gradually through supported experiences and adapted teaching.

These principles form the foundation for all future aquatic learning throughout the training pathway.

Explore the Key Concepts

Explore the Key Concepts

These core concepts frame how clubSENsational understands aquatic learning at the start of the pathway.

1

Understanding the Swimmer

  • Aquatic learning begins by observing how the swimmer experiences and responds to water.
2

Behaviour and Regulation

  • Behaviour reflects how the swimmer engages, copes and manages the aquatic environment.
3

Readiness Before Progression

  • Progression depends on control and response, not visible performance.
4

Independence as the Goal

  • Learning is guided towards confidence, self management and reduced reliance on support.
