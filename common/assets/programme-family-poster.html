The clubSENsational Swimming Programme

Three stages. Six levels. One celebrated pathway.

Your term certificate celebrates stage, level, and growth in the water. This map shows what those words mean: where your swimmer is now, and how every step forward is recognised.

Purpose

We support swimmers beyond skill completion, focusing on how they experience, respond, and function in water. Development is guided with care, not only skill delivery.

  • Functional independence
  • Skills as tools
  • Emotional and physical readiness

Values

Every decision keeps learning safe, inclusive, and meaningful, with progress that belongs to each swimmer.

  • Individual pathway
  • Safety before progression
  • Confidence through positive experience
Stage 1 Discover

Swim Confidence

Emotional readiness and sensory experience before technique: safety, comfort, and trust in the water.

Level 1 ? First Splash Turtle Calm first contact and trust
Level 2 ? Water Explorer Starfish Longer engagement, lighter prompting
Stage 2 Explore

Swim Basic

Fundamental skills for safe, confident movement: floating, balance, propulsion, and safety sequences.

Level 3 ? Floating Friend Jellyfish Float, streamline, early propulsion
Level 4 ? Ocean Navigator Ray Control, coordination, independence
Stage 3 Master

Swim Structured

Stroke refinement, rhythm, and endurance: structured swimming with integrated water safety.

Level 5 ? Independent Swimmer Dolphin Technique and sustained independence
Level 6 ? Ocean Master Whale Efficiency, fluency, endurance

Core Development Areas

On your certificate, Development this term celebrates these three areas alongside stage and level, not only swimming skills.

Regulation

How calm, safe, and emotionally available the swimmer feels in the water.

Independence

How much support is needed for position, movement, and safety.

Learning and Engagement

How the swimmer participates, attends, and responds to teaching.

How we celebrate progress

Each term certificate names the stage, level, and animal pathway, and highlights growth in regulation, independence, and engagement. Progress is individual. Returning to earlier foundations is still progress.
