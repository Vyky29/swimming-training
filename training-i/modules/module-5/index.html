        Key Ideas to Remember

        A quick recap of the principles that guide safe, structured and individual progression.

        Read each idea below, then click to confirm you've read it.

        Visual systems support learning

        Visual supports improve understanding and reduce reliance on verbal instruction.

        Structure, communication and confidence

        Structure and predictability support regulation, confidence and engagement. Visuals reduce cognitive load by making information easier to process. PixtoLearn is a structured visual system, not just a set of flashcards.

        Use visuals actively and purposefully

        Visual tools should be used actively before, during and after sessions. Communication, engagement and learning are closely connected. Visual schedules support transitions and independence. Sequencing helps swimmers move from isolated skills to meaningful outcomes. Effective teaching uses visuals to support clarity, not replace instruction.
