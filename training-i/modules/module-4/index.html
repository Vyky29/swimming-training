This module connects everything you have learned so far into a clear, staged programme. You will see how stages, levels, and core development areas work together so you can make confident decisions about where each swimmer is now and where they can move next.

          Key Ideas to Remember

          A quick recap of the principles that guide safe, structured and individual progression.

          Read each idea below, then click to confirm you've read it.

          Stages organise the journey

          Swim Confidence, Swim Basic, and Swim Structured order the path from first contact to structured swimming.

          Levels refine how you teach

          Six adaptive levels tune focus and independence within each stage — not a race through a checklist.

          Progress is individual

          Consolidation and flexible routes are normal; readiness beats calendar pace.

          Safety and wellbeing first

          Physical and emotional safety stay central from sensory work to strokes.

          Plan with a clear map

          Use stages, levels, and development areas so sessions stay achievable and meaningful.
